LP: PKA A/S (Pensionskassernes Administration A/S) (Denmark, Administration and investment-management company for the PKA group of Danish labour-market pension funds)
Why Pavilion fits: on the size test it fits easily and on the structure test it does not. PKA is one of the largest fund-of-funds buyers in Europe, with total commitments to fund-of-funds vehicles of about DKK 82bn as at 30 January 2023, and a group private-equity book of at least DKK 48bn, about USD 7bn, across the two member funds Finanstilsynet inspected at end-September 2023. Against that a US$50m ticket is rounding. The problem is who gets the money. PKA commits through IIP Denmark, the alternatives manager it owns alongside its own member funds, and IIP's mandate is the wrong half of the world: its two 2023 funds, IIP Private Funds V and IIP Venture II, took DKK 15bn on "global investment strategies focused primarily on American and European buyout funds and early-stage venture funds", and PKA's CIO framed the earlier DKK 22bn programme as supporting "small and medium-sized companies in the Nordic region, Europe and the USA". No Asia private-equity fund could be found anywhere in PKA's disclosure, and PKA publishes no fund-level private-equity list at all; the August 2026 holdings list runs 3,043 lines and names no private-equity fund. Verdict, deprioritise. PKA's fund-of-funds budget is captive to its own manager, and that manager does not do Asia.
Route to a decision: PKA's investment department prepares the papers in its own words, "Investeringsafdelingen udarbejder desuden oplaeg til bestyrelser og investeringskomiteen vedrorende nye investeringsmaessige tiltag", and an investeringskomite plus the boards of the individual member funds decide. Jon Steingrim Johnsen is Administrerende direktor of PKA A/S and CEO of the member funds, Mads Skaaning is Direktor, Investeringer, and Anders Kuhnau chairs the PKA A/S board. The legal signer is the individual pension fund, which uses its own named vehicles. Cadence is not published. The one opening is regulatory and it is time-limited: Finanstilsynet's April 2024 inspection ordered the board to set out "i hvilket omfang der kan investeres i fonde, funds-of-funds-fonde og enkeltinvesteringer samt foretages co-investeringer", so PKA is writing down its fund-of-funds authority now, and a mandate being drafted is easier to widen than one already set.
